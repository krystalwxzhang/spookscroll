# spookscroll

**What it does**

We live in an era of bottomless algorithmic feeds where standard self-control completely breaks down. Traditional screen-time limits rely on gentle nudges that users habitually swipe away. Spookscroll stops doomscrolling by using computer vision and Gemma to force instant, unavoidable self-awareness.

**🚀 What It Does**

- Behavioral Tracking: It monitors scroll velocity and gaze patterns in real-time.

- The Interruption: When it detects erratic scrolling or a catatonic, blank stare, it forcefully locks down the browser tab.

- It snaps a silent local frame of your face, triggers a full-screen dystopian overlay, and uses Gemma to generate a hyper-specific, unhinged audio-visual roast detailing exactly how much time you've wasted.

- To unlock your feed, you have to survive an active mini-game or type out an antidote phrase to prove you're conscious.
